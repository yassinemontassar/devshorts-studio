[low, steady voice] Your cache just died. [short pause] Your database is next.

Here's a normal day. Ninety-five percent of reads are answered by Redis. Postgres only sees the misses.

Then Redis restarts. [short pause] It comes back... [long pause] empty.

Now EVERY read misses. Five percent of traffic becomes a hundred percent. [short pause] That's twenty times the load... in one second.

Queries slow down. Connections pile up. The API times out, so clients retry... [exhales] and make it worse.

[calm, matter-of-fact] The fix isn't a bigger database. Coalesce requests, so one query refills each key. Put a breaker in front of Postgres. And stagger your expiry times.

[confident] Your cache isn't an optimization. [short pause] It's load-bearing.
